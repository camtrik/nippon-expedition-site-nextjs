### Added
- New fleet unit: the **Empyrean Guard (Sea Longma Riders)**, with a timed magic ability of their own, The Heavenly River Tips.
- New fleet artillery: the **Abyssal Grand Cannon**, which can switch between two magic shells, Torrent and Cloudburst.
- The Jade Sea Fleet's flagship gains a **Shipboard Stables** chain: three levels, recruiting Peasant Horsemen, then Jade Lancers, then Great Longma Riders.
- The flagship also gains a **Shipboard Sea Spirit Shrine** chain, unlocked once Serena joins the fleet: it recruits Lothern Sea Guard, Oceanids, Sea Elementals and the Merwyrm in turn, and grants ward save to the whole fleet.
- Shen Changfeng's skill tree gains **Powder and Sorcery**: more missile damage and ammunition for Fleet Gunners, Tide Guard and the Abyssal Grand Cannon, and more magic power for herself.
- New optional submod: the **[War under Heaven](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3297754796) unit compatibility patch**, which lets the Jade Sea Fleet recruit the Eastern Provinces' special units aboard the flagship.

### Changed
- Shen Changfeng's starting army is re-equipped: Grand Cannons become Abyssal Grand Cannons, and Great Longma Riders become Empyrean Guard.
- Thunderblade Hatamoto and Tachibana Naotora now have their Shiden effects.
- Companion quest pop-ups now wait for one card to be closed before the next appears.

### Fixed
- Other Cathay factions that confederate Shen Changfeng can now keep using the Cloud Sails leap.
- Grand Cannons, Jade Warriors and other vanilla Cathay units no longer carry a flickering upgrade arrow on their unit card.
- The Fleet Gunners' weapon model is no longer misaligned.
