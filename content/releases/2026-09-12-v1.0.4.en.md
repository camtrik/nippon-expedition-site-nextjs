### Added
- New generic lord: the **Fleet Admiral**, recruitable by every Cathay faction — each major city port you hold raises the recruitment cap by one — with a flagship building tree of his own.
- New optional submod: the **[WH3 Tech-tree Combination](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3117083206)** compatibility patch.

### Changed
- Shen Changfeng and the Fleet Admiral now sail the campaign map in their own dragon-boat model.
- Serena's chapter 4 finale is now a quest battle, entered straight from the teleport button in the quest panel.
- Shen Changfeng's Tidestrider mount is faster: speed up from 82 to 125. The Sea Longma comes back down, from 125 to 115.
- The lords' and heroes' unique skill lines now buff the units added by the compatibility patches as well.

### Fixed
- Turning off a faction's relocation in MCT no longer leaves that faction listed in your victory objectives.
- Tide Guard showed as Yin but could not trade Harmony with the units around them.
- Hotfix (2026-09-16): with the [War under Heaven](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3297754796) compatibility patch, recruiting on shore bypassed the unlocks and gave direct access to the Northern, Western and Southern Provinces' units.
