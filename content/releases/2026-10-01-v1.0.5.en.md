### Added
- New **Far East Seaways**: to suit the new map, the Jade Sea Fleet gets three fast sea routes.

### Updated for 9.0 and IEE's new Nippon map
- The Jade Sea Fleet's starting position has moved.
- Companion triggers: Serena now starts at Shuge Monastery, Tachibana Naotora at Jakken.
- Victory objectives have been redrawn for the new map.
- Lord relocations: Grimgor Ironhide now lands at The Great Shrine, Skarbrand the Exiled at Ukiyo.
- For other Cathay factions, the Samurai Hall line now unlocks by capturing Kaimon, and the Shrine line by capturing The Great Shrine.
- Fleet Admiral: Shuge Monastery and Tetsu Fortress count as major ports for the Jade Sea Fleet, and the opening reminder mission lets you click a city name to jump to it.

### Changed
- Sayo's Summon Divine Beast: Kirin now lasts 3 minutes.
- The Jade Sea Fleet now finds frozen, magical forest, wasteland and ocean climates suitable.
- Miko recruitment slots now open once the Shrine reaches tier 3.

### Fixed
- The Kirin summoned by Sayo was missing Thunder Echo and its heavenly lightning.
- The Alchemist could not be recruited after the fleet Watchtower was upgraded to level 2.
- Hotfix (2026-10-01): Cloudburst's double ammunition cost is reverted (it could stop the Abyssal Grand Cannon from firing); the cannon's ammunition goes from 22 to 18.
