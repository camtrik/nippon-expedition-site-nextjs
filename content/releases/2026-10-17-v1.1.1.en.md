### Hotfix (2026-10-20)

- Empyrean Guard: armour restored to 110 and total hit points restored to 8,304 (24 riders).
- Hidden debuffs reduce the autoresolve effectiveness of the Empyrean Guard and Tide Guard without changing unit stats or affecting manual battles.

### Hotfix (2026-10-19)
- Fixed armies being unable to encamp after confederating Shen Changfeng.
- Fleet Gunners: unit attribute Vanguard Deployment removed.
- Empyrean Guard:
    - Weapon strength: 22 + 58 armour-piercing → 20 + 50 armour-piercing.
    - Melee attack / melee defence: 53 / 48 → 42 / 36.
    - Hit points per model: 338 → 301 (24 riders in total: 8112 → 7224).
    - Armour: 110 → 80.
    - New passive: **Bearing of the Guard**.
- Tide Guard:
    - Melee attack / melee defence: 40 / 45 → 31 / 38.
    - Melee weapon strength: 12 + 22 armour-piercing → 11 + 20 armour-piercing.
    - New passive: **Bearing of the Guard**.
- New passive **Bearing of the Guard** (Empyrean Guard, Tide Guard): while hit points are above 25%, melee attack +10, melee defence +10 and melee weapon strength +10% (base and armour-piercing); it stops working once hit points fall to 25% or below.

### Added
- New unit: **Misaki Crow**, recruited from shrines.
- Tachibana Naotora: new mount, **Kurokumo**.
- Sayo:
    - New mount: **Sacred Steed** (level 8).
    - New mount: **Misaki Crow** (level 13).

- Ibuki: new lead-in quest, **Rumours of Goshinmori**. Once Sayo's Chapter II is complete, it points you to Goshinmori to find Ibuki; before you meet her, the Companions panel also tells you where to look.
