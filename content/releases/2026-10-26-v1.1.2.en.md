This update includes emergency fixes for several bugs. A new mechanic centered on Fleet Admirals will be coming soon.

### Main changes

- Fixed an issue that prevented companions promoted to Lords from being recruited again after recovering from wounds.
- Added Yari Cavalry, recruitable from tier 3 of the Samurai building line.
- Kaimon's Yari Dojo: construction cost 3000 → 2000, construction time 3 → 2 turns. Added an upgrade, Cavalry Training Grounds, which allows recruitment of Yari Cavalry and grants them +2 recruit rank.
- Fleet Admiral variants now have distinct skill trees. Their traits have been weakened; a dedicated mechanic will arrive in a future update.
    - Minor updates to the unique skill lines of First Aboard and Tide-Reader.
    - Order to Fire is now a female Admiral with an updated model and skill tree; Tide-Reader has an updated appearance and weapon.
- Serena, including her Lord form: Fleet Drill reduced from 3 ranks to 1, with range and reload bonuses changed from +5%/+10%/+15% each to +7% each. Battle-Hardened reduced from 3 ranks to 1, with personal melee attack and melee defence changed from +3/+6/+9 each to +5 each.
    - ⚠️ Previously learned skills may stop working in existing saves. You can back up your save and try resetting skill points; this workaround has not been verified, and there is currently no automatic migration.
- Fixed Sayo's Mirrored Reflection skill: it now reduces Winds of Magic costs for all spells by 15%.
- Added Stalk to Akatsuki-shu (Katana).
