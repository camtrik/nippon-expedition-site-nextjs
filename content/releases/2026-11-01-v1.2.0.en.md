### Added

- New "Jade Sea Admiralty" mechanic:
    - Alongside Shen Changfeng, you can recruit 4 additional unique Fleet Admirals by completing their recruitment conditions (exclusive to the Jade Sea Fleet).
    - Each Admiral has a dedicated growth track; completing its milestones unlocks reserve army replenishment and various bonuses.
    - Each Admiral specializes in their own signature troop types and bonuses:
        - Shen Changfeng: excels at commanding Guard units.
        - Vanguard Marshal (First Aboard): excels at commanding infantry units.
        - Ordnance Master (Order to Fire): excels at commanding gunpowder and artillery units.
        - Cavalry Marshal (Tide-Reader): excels at commanding cavalry units.
        - Yin Ling: excels at commanding monsters and war beasts.
    - For other Cathay factions, Fleet Admirals no longer scale infinitely with port count; instead, they are capped at one each of First Aboard, Order to Fire, and Tide-Reader.
- New Legendary Lord: Yin Ling, the Beastmaster Admiral:
    - Excels at commanding sea monsters and has 5 mounts.
- New units: Tamed Cold Ones, Leviathan, Boarding Axe Guard, Golden-Eyed Beast Riders, Pelagic Wyrm, Jade Sea Sailors (Dual Blades):
    - Replaced the starting Sea Longma Riders (Empyrean Guard) with Golden-Eyed Beast Riders.
- New sea-beast buildings:
    - Shore landmarks "Beast Cliffs" and "Abyss of Sea Beasts" (Kichikujima).
    - Yin Ling's shipboard building chain "Menagerie Hold".

### Changed

- Adjusted the mounts of the three Fleet Admiral seats.
- Adjusted stats and skills for Tachibana Naotora, Ibuki, and Serena.
- Streamlined the Admiral horde building tree, moving units from the main building into more fitting chains; added the "Admiral's Guard" chain.

### Fixed

- Fixed the Abyssal Grand Cannon not benefiting from stun-related technology bonuses.
- Attempted a fix for multiplayer campaign crashes (further verification needed).
