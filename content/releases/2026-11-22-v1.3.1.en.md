### Added

- Other Cathay factions can recruit Yin Ling, the Beastmaster Admiral, after capturing Shi-Wu. This also applies to existing saves.

### Changes

- Other Cathay factions permanently unlock the corresponding Nippon buildings and units when they, a military ally, or one of their vassals holds the required settlement.
- Standard Fleet Admiral ships now have 10 building slots (up from 8), enough for the main cabin and all nine other building chains.
- Teppo Samurai sword armour-piercing damage has been reduced from 10 to 8. The sword loses its +14 bonus versus infantry and its splash attack, which could hit up to five targets.

### Fixes

- Fixed missing recruit rank bonuses for certain lords recruited from Fleet Admiral ships.
- Added the standard Mentor skill to Shen Changfeng, the hero forms of Tachibana Naotora and Ibuki, and the lord forms of Tachibana Naotora, Ibuki, Sayo, and Serena.

### Jade Sea Fleet Unit Pack

- Complete the mission at Fuchow to recruit Ryuka. Her merchant ship can recruit the Shiokaze Armed Company (Hand Cannons), the Shiokaze Armed Company (Flamethrowers), and the Shiokaze Land Ship.
- The standalone pack also includes the extra admiral ship slots, the shipboard recruit rank fix, the Mentor skills, and the Teppo Samurai sword changes. [Standalone Unit Pack](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3831125635)
