You only need this mod if you are also subscribed to **[Walking with Dragons (DEER24 Cathay)](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2789903784)**.

- 2026-10-19 New event chain, **Aid for Fu-Chow**: once Serena's Chapter I is complete, if Fu-Chow has fallen to a foreign enemy and the treasury holds at least 5000, the *Fu-Chow in Peril* dilemma appears.
    - Choose **Relieve Fu-Chow**: 5000 is deducted; you gain Supply Line Support for 1 turn and Forces Sent to Aid for 5 turns. A Fleet Admiral's army (5 Jade Sea Warriors, 5 Fleet Gunners, 1 Abyssal Grand Cannon) appears off Fu-Chow, war is declared on its current owner, and the *Retake Fu-Chow* mission is issued.
    - Taking Fu-Chow grants +2500 gold and Fu-Chow Restored for 5 turns (diplomacy with Cathay +30, Fu-Chow growth +30).
    - After relieving Fu-Chow the Jade Sea Fleet is no longer exempt from the State of the Celestial Empire, the loss of Wei-Jin and the Fifteen Golden Tablets, and can trigger Walking with Dragons' influence storyline.
    - Choose **Focus on the Expedition**: All Hands to the Expedition for 5 turns, everything else unchanged.
- 2026-10-16 Losing Cathay's heartland no longer causes a public order penalty.
- 2026-10-16 The Jade Sea Fleet can now trigger Chenfeng's and Li Su's battle quests.
- 2026-10-16 Some of Walking with Dragons' unit bonuses now also apply to the Jade Sea Fleet's new units.
- 2026-10-06 Released. The Jade Sea Fleet and every Cathay faction can confederate each other again, including the Okumoto and Sanyo Clans on Nippon. Confederation between other Cathay factions still follows Walking with Dragons' rules.
    - ⚠️ This patch requires Walking with Dragons; the game will not start without it. Decide before starting a campaign, and do not unsubscribe from Walking with Dragons mid-campaign.
- 2026-10-06 The Jade Sea Fleet can recruit the 14 Regiments of Renown that Walking with Dragons makes available from the start of the campaign.
    - This part used to live in the Cathay Mechanics Compatibility Patch and has moved here. Regiments that Walking with Dragons unlocks through its own quest chains are not included.
- 2026-10-06 The Jade Sea Fleet is exempt from 天朝局势 (the State of the Celestial Empire).
- 2026-10-06 The top bar shows 军功 (Merits), and 10 of the mod's own units — Yari Samurai, Teppo Samurai, Thunderblade Hatamoto and more — can be upgraded with them.
